Voice Architecture & Engines

1
F5-TTS (Flow-Matching Diffusion)

Ultra-realistic non-autoregressive voice cloning. Delivers natural human pauses, breathing, and pitch inflection.

2
LuxTTS (150x Real-Time Ultra-Fast Diffusion)

Generates high-fidelity cloned speech in 0.15s–0.25s on GPU, offering minimal latency for rapid-fire telephone exchanges.

3
Amazon Polly (US Male & Female Native Telephony)

Native Twilio carrier synthesis with 0.0s GPU overhead. Rock-solid fallback for standard business calls.

4
One-Click Activation & Cross-Sync

Setting an active voice instantly updates Twilio telephony, the browser Call Simulator, and pre-warms GPU audio greetings.
